This page explains what information karachihijama.com collects and what happens to it. Last updated: 26 September 2026.

Who we are

Shaheen Shafi Unani Clinic & Hijama Center, 20/32-33 Sardar Muhammad Ali Road, Nishterabad, Model Colony, Karachi 75100, Pakistan. Phone / WhatsApp: +92 322 2905560.

Website statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google, to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city or country level), the type of device and browser, how people found us (for example a search engine or an AI assistant), and whether someone taps a WhatsApp or call button or uses the booking form. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. We do not send your name, phone number, email address or message to Google Analytics. We may use these measurements to see which adverts work (for example Google Ads). You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Booking by WhatsApp or phone

The WhatsApp buttons and the booking form open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Nothing you type in the form is stored on this website. If you send the message, it is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under WhatsApp's own terms, and we see it in our WhatsApp account so we can reply and arrange your appointment. Please share only the health details needed to book safely.

Email copies of booking requests

If you add an email address to the booking form, your name, phone, email, chosen service, message and the page you were on are also sent to the clinic by email. This goes through Google (a Google Apps Script that forwards the request to the clinic's Gmail inbox). We use it only to reply to your enquiry. If you leave the email field empty, no email copy is sent.

Other services the pages load

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the homepage shows an embedded Google Map. Loading these can share your IP address and browser details with Google. Your language choice (English or Urdu) is remembered in your own browser only.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your messages or email deleted, contact us on WhatsApp or call +92 322 2905560. We keep booking messages only as long as needed to look after you and answer follow-up questions.
