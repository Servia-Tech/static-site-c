The complaint we hear most from PECHS is not dramatic — it is the neck, the upper back and the shoulders. This is an area full of people who spend nine or ten hours at a desk or behind a shop counter, then sit in traffic on Shahrah-e-Faisal, then answer messages on a phone at home. That posture load settles into the same few muscles. Patients tell us a Hijama session leaves those areas feeling looser and lighter, and many book it as an occasional reset rather than as treatment for a named condition.
